# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-10
- New overlay product board: buttons with no link and product cards open a full-screen
  festive board with 3D-tilt product cards, quick view, gallery, quantity and add-to-cart.
- New interactive festive scenes (Ramadan lanterns + crescent, 3D snowballs, confetti,
  golden stars) reacting to mouse, touch and phone tilt.
- Hero: new "interactive background" setting.
- New component: Festive Layer (page-wide falling snow / stars / confetti with on/off button).
- Buttons: new setting to open the board or scroll to the products.
- Templates: Ramadan uses lanterns + snow; White Friday and National Day use confetti.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10
- First release: 10 components (hero, countdown, product spotlight, problem/solution,
  benefits, testimonials, comparison, FAQ, trust badges, final CTA + sticky bar).
- 4 templates: Product Launch (default), Ramadan, White Friday, Saudi National Day.
- Arabic-first RTL layouts, Arabic/English text, automatic contrast correction.
